## What it does

- **One tap per set.** Each set is prefilled from your last session. If you hit the same numbers, logging it is one tap.
- **A rest timer that starts on its own.** It starts when you log a working set. A Live Activity counts it down on your lock screen, and FlowState alerts you when rest is over, even from another app, if you allow notifications.
- **Personal records for every lift.** FlowState flags a new record when you log it, and keeps each lift's best in one place.
- **Starter templates.** Push, pull, legs, upper body and full body are ready to add and change. You can make your own too.
- **Apple Health.** If you allow it, FlowState saves your finished workouts to Health and reads your body weight from it.
- **Works offline.** Every set is saved on your iPhone first, so logging never waits for a signal.
- **Backed up to your account.** Sign in with Apple or email, and a new iPhone gets your history back.
- **Free, with no ads.** No tracking either. [Read the privacy policy](privacy).
