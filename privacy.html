FlowState — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

FlowState is a workout tracker for iPhone. This policy describes what it collects, where that goes, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read rather than to be defensible.

The short version

  • You can use FlowState without an account. Everything about training — logging sets, the

rest timer, your history, templates, records — works signed out, and nothing leaves your phone unless you sign in.

  • If you sign in, your training data is backed up to our server so you don't lose it with

your phone, and so it can appear on more than one device.

  • Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising. There is no advertising SDK, no

analytics SDK, and no third-party tracking of any kind in this app.

  • You can delete your account and everything on the server from inside the app, at any

time, without asking anyone.

What is stored on your phone

Whether or not you have an account:

  • Workouts, exercises, sets, weights, reps, rest times and any notes you add
  • Templates you create
  • Profile details you choose to give: name, birth date, gender, height, body weight, training

goal, experience level, days per week, and equipment you have access to

  • App preferences — units, appearance, default rest, whether RPE is shown

Birth date, gender and body weight are health data. They are optional. FlowState works without them; they exist because strength standards and bodyweight-exercise volume depend on them.

What is sent to our server, and when

Only if you sign in. Signing in is the only feature in FlowState that requires a network connection.

When you do, the data listed above is copied to our backend (Supabase, hosted in the United States) so it can be restored and synced. It is stored under your account and is readable only by you: every table enforces row-level security keyed to your account, so no other user of FlowState can read your rows.

Your email address comes from Sign in with Apple or from the email you register with. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only ever see Apple's relay address.

What is not collected

  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking
  • No location
  • No contacts, photos, or microphone
  • Nothing is sold or shared with third parties for their own purposes

Crash and hang reports come from Apple's MetricKit and stay on your device unless you choose to send them to us with a support email.

Apple Health

FlowState can write your finished workouts to Apple Health, and read your body weight from it, only if you grant permission. That data moves between FlowState and Health on your device. Health data read from Apple Health is never used for advertising and is never shared.

You can withdraw this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Deleting your data

  • Sign out — the server keeps your backup; your phone keeps its copy.
  • Delete account (Settings → Account) — your account and everything backed up to it are

permanently erased. The workouts on your phone are kept, and are no longer backed up.

  • Delete the app — everything on the phone goes with it.

You can export everything at any time from Settings → Data → Export, which produces a single JSON file you keep.

If you are in the EU or UK, this covers your rights to access, export and erasure. If you would rather ask a person, use the address below.

Children

FlowState is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the app will say so before the change takes effect.
